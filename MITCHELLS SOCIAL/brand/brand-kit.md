## Voice

Warm, hometown, a little playful and hungry: "southern comfort deliciousness", "Amazing OMELETS", "shrimp goes with everything", "made for you to crave". Short punchy lines, food-first. Family pride without inventing family stories.

- Do: name the dish, lead with "fresh, never frozen" and "made to order", use the Space Coast / US-1 hooks, invite people in ("7am-2pm every day").
- Don't: prices, invented deals or giveaways, star ratings or review counts, "Cocoa Village", stories about Erica or the family that Frank hasn't given us, claims like "best in Brevard" as fact (the site's own taglines are OK when quoted as a tagline).
